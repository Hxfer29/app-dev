# 🎬 My Favorite Anime Movie: *Your Name (Kimi no Na wa)*

![Your Name Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/0b/Your_Name_poster.png)

## 🌠 Overview
*Your Name* is a **Japanese animated romantic fantasy film** written and directed by **Makoto Shinkai**.  
It tells the story of two teenagers, **Mitsuha Miyamizu** and **Taki Tachibana**, who mysteriously begin to swap bodies despite living in different towns and times.

---

## 👥 Main Characters
- **Mitsuha Miyamizu** – A high school girl from the countryside who dreams of living in Tokyo.  
- **Taki Tachibana** – A boy from Tokyo who works part-time in an Italian restaurant.  
- **Yotsuha Miyamizu** – Mitsuha’s younger sister.  
- **Okudera-senpai** – Taki’s kind coworker.

---

## 💖 Favorite Quote
